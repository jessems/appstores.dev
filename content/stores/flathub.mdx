Flathub has emerged as the de facto Linux app store, supported by major distributions including Fedora, Ubuntu, and Linux Mint. Its Flatpak format provides sandboxed, distro-agnostic packaging. With growing support from commercial developers (including Spotify, Slack, Discord), it's bridging the app gap on Linux desktop.
