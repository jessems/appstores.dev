The Roku Channel Store is the #1 streaming platform in the US by hours streamed. With a unique BrightScript programming language and SceneGraph framework, channels range from major services (Netflix, Disney+, YouTube) to niche content and casual games. Roku's ad-supported model has made it increasingly attractive for free content distribution.
